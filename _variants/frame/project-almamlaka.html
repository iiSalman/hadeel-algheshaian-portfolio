Plate I — Threshold Najdi pendant work above brick coursing.
On atmosphere

A building that would feel inevitable.

The first move was the threshold: a deep porch of brick coursing and tasselled pendants that does the labour of an actual masonry wall, even though it is only forty centimetres of frame.

On material

Inside, the strategy is reduction. A single colour story — terracotta, brass, bone — and a single rule about light: it falls, never glares.

The carpet is a continuous deep red, hand-knotted to deaden footfall, so the room sounds older than it looks.
