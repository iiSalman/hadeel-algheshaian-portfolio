On atmosphere

Tone, not graphic.

Reception, waiting, manager's office, shared workfloor. The brief asked for restraint that still reads as branded; the answer was tone — a single olive, a single brass, a single oak — repeated everywhere.

On material

No logos on walls. The branding is in the way the rooms behave: how light arrives, what gets reflected, how the air smells.
