§ Brief

The plan does little; the air does the work.

A square of stone, four shaded edges, one tree at the centre. The plan is almost diagrammatic — and the courtyard depends entirely on what arrives from outside it: the heat of the day, the angle of light, evening cool, and the quiet that gathers when nothing in particular is happening.

The composition is sensitive to the hours: a low sun on the west wall in the late afternoon, a deep shadow at noon, a slow blue at dusk. We chose stone of varied warmth across the four edges, so that the same hour reads differently depending on which wall you are sitting against.
