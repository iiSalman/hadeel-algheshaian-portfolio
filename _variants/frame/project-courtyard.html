Plate I — Stone Square plan, four edges.
On atmosphere

The plan does little; the air does the work.

A square of stone, four shaded edges, one tree at the centre. The plan is almost diagrammatic — and the courtyard depends on what arrives from outside it: heat, light, evening cool.

On material

The composition is sensitive to the hours: low sun on the west wall in the late afternoon, deep shadow at noon, a slow blue at dusk.
